# Domain interaction checks

Read only the relevant section.

## Combat and abilities

- Target acquisition, aim assistance, range, line of sight, team rules, and friendly fire.
- Startup, active, recovery, buffering, cancellation, interruption, cooldown, and resource cost.
- Hit detection, invulnerability, armor, stagger, immunity, simultaneous hits, and effect stacking.
- Projectile or area ownership, lifetime, collision, prediction, reconciliation, and duplicate damage.
- Telegraph, hit confirmation, miss, blocked, resisted, and unavailable feedback.

## Traversal and manipulation

- Approach, facing, reach, clearance, hand/foot placement, camera, and navigation alignment.
- Reservation and exclusivity for ladders, doors, seats, ledges, pushables, or narrow passages.
- Entry, loop, exit, interruption, falling, obstruction, moving platforms, and invalid destination.
- Collision changes, root motion, warping, animation mismatch, and recovery location.
- Alternate input, timing assistance, reduced camera motion, and failure recovery.

## Inventory, crafting, and economy

- Ownership, capacity, stacking, uniqueness, quality, durability, and locked/reserved items.
- Currency authority, price calculation, discounts, taxes, refunds, rounding, and insufficient funds.
- Atomic transfer, duplicate requests, rollback, disconnection, save/load, and transaction history.
- Recipe eligibility, ingredient choice, output placement, overflow, cancellation, and partial progress.
- Reveal, comparison, confirmation, error, and destructive-action feedback.

## Dialogue, quests, and narrative

- Speaker eligibility, interruption, re-entry, skip, timeout, and concurrent world events.
- Choice availability, hidden information, irreversible choices, confirmation, and consequence preview.
- Quest-state guards, branch convergence, failure, abandonment, restart, and save compatibility.
- Localization expansion, reading time, subtitles, input remapping, text-to-speech, and voiced/unvoiced fallback.
- Cinematic camera or movement constraints and return of player control.

## AI interactions

- Perception, memory, intent, target selection, reservation, and role coordination.
- Preconditions, utility or priority, cooldown, abort conditions, and fallback behavior.
- Player interruption, environmental invalidation, nav failure, unreachable target, and deadlock recovery.
- Readable intent, state feedback, fairness, difficulty scaling, and deterministic test hooks.

## Multiplayer and shared-world behavior

- Client/server authority, ownership, prediction, validation, replication, and reconciliation.
- Simultaneous requests, race resolution, reservation, idempotency, and latency tolerance.
- Join-in-progress, disconnect, host migration, reconnect, rollback, and stale messages.
- Cheating/trust boundaries, privacy, moderation, and platform identity constraints.
- Spectator or non-owner feedback and accessibility under network delay.
